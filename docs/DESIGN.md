## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Audience
The design system establishes a high-performance athletic identity tailored for competitive padel leagues, tournament directors, club managers, and active players. It captures the crisp, architectural clarity of contemporary panoramic glass courts under bright Mediterranean daylight. The tone balances tournament-grade rigor with club hospitality: authoritative, pristine, dynamic, and community-driven.

The audience encompasses weekend tournament players submitting match reports courtside on mobile phones, spectators tracking real-time ladder standings, and league administrators managing multi-division fixtures on desktop workstations. The emotional reaction must evoke focus, athletic prestige, effortless accuracy, and zero visual friction under harsh outdoor sun.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Expression
The aesthetic fuses **Modern Athletic Luxury** with **Functional Light Minimalism**:
- **Aura-Inspired Luminous Canvas:** Pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) floating over a clean slate base (`#F8FAFC`) with hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`), creating high ambient brightness without harsh contrast.
- **Athletic Vitality:** Emerald turf vibrance paired with electric cyan accents delivers kinetic energy to buttons, live pulses, and active set states.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Dynamic, bold display headers paired with systematic tabular typography ensure rapid data comprehension in bright environments.
- **Glassmorphic Touch Surfaces:** Soft frosted layers (`16px` backdrop blur with semi-transparent surfaces) bring depth to floating sticky shells, captain verification cards, and digital signature sheets.

## Colors

### Color Strategy & Hierarchy
The palette anchors on luminous high-key grounds and athletic chromatics:
- **Primary Turf Emerald (`#059669`):** Represents the heart of the court. Used for primary call-to-actions, victory tags, verified tournament stamps, and active selection states.
- **Secondary Cyan Pulse (`#0891B2`):** Serves as an energetic partner in directional gradients (`linear-gradient(135deg, #10B981 0%, #06B6D4 100%)`) for primary buttons and team portal interactive accents.
- **Tertiary Court Blue (`#2563EB`):** Dedicated to live tie-break banners, administrative filters, and match fixture indicators.
- **Neutral Slate (`#0F172A`):** Used for typography, providing near-black slate density that prevents ink wash under sunlight without the starkness of pure black.

### Semantic Match States
Strict color-coded governance is preserved across all micro-frontends:
- **Confirmed / Win (`#10B981`):** Signals settled sets, verified match scores, and approved captain submissions. Always paired with emerald tinted backgrounds (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`).
- **Pending / In Review (`#D97706`):** Marks unplayed rounds, pending captain approval, and Punto de Oro sudden-death moments.
- **Disputed / Issue (`#DC2626`):** Identifies disputed sets, scoring discrepancies, and administrative intervention alerts.
- **Cancelled / Neutral (`#64748B`):** Denotes walkovers, postponed matches, or bye rounds.

### Surface System
Surfaces rely on layer separation over shadows: canvas background sits at `#F8FAFC`, raised cards sit at `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, and floating components utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a backdrop blur of `16px`. Hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`) frame all interactive panels cleanly.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings & Roles
The typographic hierarchy merges athletic character with tabular utility:
- **Headlines & Display (`Outfit`):** A geometric, wide-aperture sans-serif imparting speed and modernity. Used for large tournament hero headings, section introductions, team names, and scoreboard digits. Snug tracking (`-0.02em`) creates visual cohesion in headers.
- **Body & Data Tables (`Inter`):** A high x-height workhorse delivering reading comfort across dense multi-column standings, match histories, and form fields. It eliminates visual fatigue when cross-referencing sets and point differences.
- **Micro-Labels & Status Badges (`Outfit` / `Inter` Uppercase):** All category tags, table headers, and status flags apply letter tracking of `+0.05em` to `+0.1em` for instant legibility at reduced sizes.

### Courtside Scoreboard Rules
Numeric point counters (`15`, `30`, `40`, `V`) use tabular numeral alignments in heavy weights (`800` or `900`) centered within designated bounding containers. The active serving game digit is rendered at `fontWeight: 900`, ensuring clear contrast against historical set columns.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model & Container Structure
The design system implements a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with a maximum content container of `1240px`:
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, and `2rem` (`32px`) exterior page margins. Main dashboard cards, KPI metrics, and standing tables comfortably occupy 3-column, 4-column, or full-width spans.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, with responsive multi-tier grids collapsing into 2-column formats for match summaries and stats.
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters, and `1rem` (`16px`) exterior margins. Data tables stack into priority cards, and courtside scoring transforms into a single-column layout.

### Micro-Frontend Shell Variations
1. **Public & Portal Shell (`rpm-rankings`, `rpm-admin`, `rpm-teams`):** Uses a centered container with a floating sticky glass navigation bar (`top: 1rem`) spanning `max-width: 1240px`.
2. **Courtside Live Mode (`rpm-live`):** Strips away navigation menus, breadcrumbs, and footers. The grid locks to a focused viewport width (`max-width: 900px`) centered vertically and horizontally to prevent accidental gestures during active play.

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows an exact `4px` base unit:
- `space-xs` (`4px`): Micro padding inside tags and icon-to-label gaps.
- `space-sm` (`8px`): Compact component padding, list item gaps.
- `space-md` (`16px`): Standard form field gaps, table cell padding, card inner margins.
- `space-lg` (`24px`): Card padding and layout row separation.
- `space-xl` (`32px`): Section gaps and dashboard container dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Strategy
Elevation avoids dark, heavy drops. It is built using **light-refracting multi-tier ambient shadows** combined with **frosted glass translucency** (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`):

- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F8FAFC` background. Completely shadow-free.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & PrimeNG Panels):** High-opacity pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and diffused dual ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Interactive Cards & Hover States):** Lifts interactive match fixture cards on hover:
  - `transform: translateY(-2px)`
  - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
- **Tier 3 (Floating Navbars & Overlays):** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) with a blur filter:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
  - `border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`
- **Tier 4 (Dialogs & Modals):** Form dialogs (Match Result Entry, Incident Ticket) utilize centered presentation with a deep ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`
  - Backdrop dimming layer: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

### Chromatic Glows
To convey live status, elements use low-opacity chromatic glows instead of solid strokes:
- **Victory / Active Live Match:** `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.20)`
- **Focus Rings:** `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`

## Shapes

### Corner Curvature Language
The shape hierarchy is set to `2` (Rounded), providing balanced corners that feel modern, approachable, and ergonomic for touch interactions:
- **Default Elements (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** PrimeNG input fields, standard buttons, dropdown selectors, and table container corners.
- **Intermediate Cards (`1rem` / `16px`):** Content cards, fixture summaries, PrimeNG dialog windows, and the sticky glass navigation shell.
- **Large Focus Elements (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Courtside live scorecards and hero banner highlight boxes.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Status badges, podium ranking position circles, round ball serving indicators, and floating filter chips.
